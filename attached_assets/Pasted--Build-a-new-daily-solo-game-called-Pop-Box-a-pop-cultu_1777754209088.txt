How It Works:
A 3×3 grid (9 squares total). Top row has 3 category labels. Left column has 3 category labels. Each square is the intersection of two categories. Player types a celebrity/artist name that fits BOTH categories for that square.

Show the 3×3 grid with category labels

After completing, show results and rarity score
