## **BUILD POP OR DROP GAME - REPLIT AGENT PROMPT (REVISED)**

---

**Tell Replit Agent:**

"Build a new daily solo game called Pop or Drop - a Higher or Lower style guessing game with pop culture metrics.

**CRITICAL: Use Y2K design aesthetic - bright colors, thick black borders, comic book text styling.**

**How It Works:**

Player sees two items side by side. Left item shows its metric number. Right item's number is hidden. Player guesses if the right item's number is HIGHER or LOWER than the left. Keep playing until wrong guess. ONE ATTEMPT PER DAY. Score = streak length.

**IMPORTANT: Categories are MIXED - you might compare Taylor Swift's Spotify listeners to the population of France, then compare France's population to LeBron James' salary. The categories blend together randomly for maximum chaos and difficulty.**

**Game Flow:**

1. Show first item with its number displayed
   Example: "Taylor Swift - 95M Spotify monthly listeners"

2. Show second item with number hidden (could be ANY category)
   Example: "Population of Canada - ???"

3. Player taps either:
   - "HIGHER ⬆" button (guess Canada has MORE than 95M)
   - "LOWER ⬇" button (guess Canada has LESS than 95M)

4. Reveal answer with animation:
   - If correct: "Canada - 39M ✓ LOWER! Streak: 1"
   - If wrong: "Canada - 39M ✗ LOWER! Final Streak: 0 - GAME OVER"

5. If correct, Canada becomes the new baseline (39M now shown on left)
   New completely random item appears on right (could be NBA salary, Instagram followers, box office gross, anything)
   Player guesses again

6. Continue until player guesses wrong - GAME OVER, no retry until tomorrow

7. Show final streak score and leaderboard

**One Attempt Per Day:**

- Player gets ONE try per day at the daily challenge
- When they guess wrong, game ends - no restart until tomorrow
- Everyone gets the same sequence of items each day
- Daily leaderboard shows who got the longest streak
- Next day at midnight, new sequence available

**Mixed Categories - No Separation:**

All items from all categories go into one big pool. The daily sequence randomly pulls from this pool, so you never know what comparison is coming next.

Items can have wildly different metric types but same scale numbers:
- "Taylor Swift - 95M Spotify listeners" vs "Population of Germany - ???" (both in millions)
- "Eiffel Tower - 1,083 feet tall" vs "LeBron James - ??? points scored" (both in thousands/similar scale)
- "Titanic opening weekend - $28M" vs "Cristiano Ronaldo annual salary - ???" (both in millions)

The game automatically displays the metric type for each item so players understand what they're comparing.
