## **GUESS THE YEAR - UPDATED WITH TAP TO REVEAL**

---

**Tell Replit Agent:**

"Build a new daily solo game called Guess the Year - a game where players guess which year pop culture events happened.

**CRITICAL: Use Y2K design aesthetic - bright colors, thick black borders, comic book text styling.**

**How It Works:**

Each day shows 3 hints about events from the same year. Player sees the first hint immediately, then can reveal more hints if needed. Player types the year after seeing as few hints as possible.

**Game Flow:**

1. Show first hint immediately (already revealed): "Seahawks beat Patriots 29-13 in Super Bowl"
2. Show hint boxes 2 and 3 as locked/unrevealed with "TAP TO REVEAL" button on each
3. Show year input box (player types 4-digit year: 2026)
4. Two main actions available:
   - "SUBMIT GUESS" (big, bright, Y2K style)
   - "TAP TO REVEAL" on hint 2 box (if not revealed yet)

5. If player submits guess:
   - Correct: Show success + share results
   - Wrong: Show "Not quite! Try another year or reveal another hint"
   - Can keep guessing or reveal more hints

6. If player clicks "TAP TO REVEAL" on hint 2:
   - Hint 2 box opens to show: "Olivia Dean wins Best New Artist at Grammys"
   - Hint 2 stays visible
   - Hint 3 still shows "TAP TO REVEAL"
   - Can guess or reveal hint 3

7. If needed, click "TAP TO REVEAL" on hint 3:
   - Show third hint: "Project Hail Mary by Andy Weir released"
   - All three hints now visible
   - Must guess now (no more hints to reveal)

**Styling:**
- Hint 1: Always revealed, colorful background (bright color), thick black border
- Hint 2 & 3 unrevealed: Grayed out or different color background, shows "🔒 TAP TO REVEAL" as clickable text/button
- Once revealed: Hint boxes flip or animate to show content, same bright colorful style as hint 1
- Each hint gets different bright color (hot pink, electric orange, cyan, lime green)
- Year input: large, centered, easy to type
- Submit button: huge, colorful, Y2K style

**Scoring System:**

Track which hint you solved it on:
- Guessed on Hint 1 only = 🏆 Perfect! (3 points)
- Guessed on Hint 2 = ⭐ Great! (2 points)
- Guessed on Hint 3 = ✓ Nice! (1 point)
- Couldn't guess = 😔 The answer was [YEAR]

**Hint Content Categories:**

Mix different types of events for the 3 hints each day:

**Sports:**
- Super Bowl winner + score
- NBA Finals winner
- World Cup winner
- Olympics host city
- Major championship wins

**Music:**
- Grammy winners (Album/Song/Artist of the Year, Best New Artist)
- Major album releases
- Billboard #1 songs
- Artist deaths
- Concert tours

**Movies/TV:**
- Best Picture Oscar winner
- Major movie releases with box office
- TV show premieres/finales
- Streaming launches

**Tech/Apps:**
- App launches (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
- iPhone/device releases
- Social media milestones

**Celebrity/Culture:**
- Celebrity marriages/divorces
- Royal weddings
- Major celebrity moments
- Viral moments/memes

**Books:**
- Major book releases
- Literary awards

**Daily Puzzle Structure:**

Each day has 3 hints from the SAME year:
- Hint 1: Most specific/hardest (requires deep knowledge) - SHOWN IMMEDIATELY
- Hint 2: Medium difficulty (broader audience might know) - TAP TO REVEAL
- Hint 3: Easiest/most famous event from that year - TAP TO REVEAL

Example for 2014:
- Hint 1 (shown): "Germany beats Argentina 1-0 in World Cup Final"
- Hint 2 (tap to reveal): "Pharrell's 'Happy' is #1 song"
- Hint 3 (tap to reveal): "Guardians of the Galaxy movie released"

**After Correct Guess:**

Show celebration screen:
- "Correct! It was [YEAR]!"
- Display which hint you solved it on with icon
- Show all 3 hints revealed (even if player didn't see them all)
- Share button
- Stats: your streak, average hints used

**After Wrong Guess:**

- "Not quite! Try again or reveal another hint"
- Player can keep guessing different years
- Player can tap to reveal next hint
- No penalty for wrong guesses (only tracked: which hint you eventually solved it on)

**Stats Tracking:**

Store in localStorage:
- Current streak (consecutive days played)
- Perfect streak (consecutive days guessed on hint 1)
- Average hints needed
- Total games played
- Best performance (how many hint-1 solves)
- Distribution: X% solved on hint 1, Y% on hint 2, Z% on hint 3

**Content Creation:**

Create at least 100 daily puzzles covering years from 1990-2026. Each puzzle needs:
- 3 hints from same year
- Difficulty progression (hard → medium → easy)
- Mix of categories
- Verified accurate dates

**Year Range:**
Focus on years with lots of memorable pop culture:
- 1990s: 1990-1999
- 2000s: 2000-2009
- 2010s: 2010-2019
- 2020s: 2020-2026

**Difficulty Balance:**
- Recent years (2020-2026): Easier (people remember)
- 2010s: Medium
- 2000s: Medium-hard
- 1990s: Harder (nostalgic for millennials, new for Gen Z)

**Homepage Integration:**

Add Guess the Year to Daily Games section showing:
- Game name "GUESS THE YEAR" with calendar icon
- Completion checkmark if played today
- Your score (🏆/⭐/✓) if completed today

**Mobile Optimization:**

- Hint boxes stack vertically, full width
- "TAP TO REVEAL" buttons are large and easy to tap
- Number input optimized for mobile keyboards
- Smooth flip/reveal animations when hints unlock
- All buttons minimum 48px tall for easy tapping

**Animation:**

When player taps "TAP TO REVEAL":
- Hint box flips or slides open
- Content fades in
- Small celebration (sparkle effect)
- Lock icon disappears

**Make the first hint challenging enough that most people need at least 2 hints, but not impossible. Create satisfying progression where hints get progressively easier/more obvious.**"
