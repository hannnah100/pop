Content for Each Game:
THREE STRIKES
Title: Three Strikes
Tagline: Name all the things before you strike out
How to Play:

You get a category (like "Marvel Movies" or "Taylor Swift Albums")
Grid shows hint boxes with clues (like release year or collaborator)
Type answers to reveal boxes
Each correct answer reveals a box (turns green)
Each wrong answer gives you a strike (❌)
3 strikes and you're out - game over
Goal: Complete the entire grid without 3 strikes

Tips:

Hints help narrow it down
Spelling counts (but we're lenient with typos)
Numbers work in answers ("1989", "Iron Man 2")

Share your results with friends!

THE SKINNY
Title: The Skinny
Tagline: Get the inside scoop with this pop culture crossword
How to Play:

A 3×9 crossword grid (tall and skinny)
All clues are pop culture themed (celebrities, movies, shows, music, apps, memes)
Click a cell to start typing
Use arrow keys or tap to move between cells
Timer tracks how fast you complete it
Fill in all the answers to win

Tips:

Numbers in parentheses show letter count
Across and Down clues intersect
Pop culture knowledge is your superpower here

Share your completion time!

POP BOX
Title: Pop Box
Tagline: Match celebrities to category crossovers
How to Play:

You get a 3×3 grid with categories on top and left side
Each square needs a celebrity/artist who fits BOTH categories
Example: "Has collabed with Drake" + "Won a Grammy" = name someone who did both
Type a name for each square
Green = correct (fits both categories)
Red = wrong (doesn't fit one or both)
Goal: Get all 9 squares correct

Scoring:

Rarity matters! Uncommon correct answers score higher
If everyone says the same answer, you get less points
Be creative with your answers

One puzzle per day - same for everyone!

POP OR DROP
Title: Pop or Drop
Tagline: Higher or lower? Test your pop culture instincts
How to Play:

See two items side by side
Left item shows its number (followers, population, salary, etc.)
Right item's number is hidden
Guess: Is the right item HIGHER ⬆ or LOWER ⬇?
If correct, keep going with a new comparison
If wrong, game over - your streak ends
Categories mix randomly (compare Spotify listeners to stadium capacity!)

Rules:

ONE attempt per day - no retries
Everyone gets the same sequence worldwide
Compete on the global leaderboard
Aim for the longest streak possible

Share your streak!

GUESS THE YEAR
Title: Guess the Year
Tagline: When did that happen? Test your timeline knowledge
How to Play:

You get 3 hints about events from the same year
First hint appears immediately
Type the year (4 digits)
If you don't know, tap to reveal the next hint
Use as few hints as possible to solve it

Scoring:

Solved on Hint 1 = 🏆 Perfect! (3 points)
Solved on Hint 2 = ⭐ Great! (2 points)
Solved on Hint 3 = ✓ Nice! (1 point)

Tips:

Hints go from hardest → easiest
Think about what else happened that year
Use hints strategically

Brag about your hint count!
