Decorative Elements to Add:

Organic blob shapes (like Koala Color)
Hand-drawn doodle illustrations: smiley faces, stars, lightning bolts, hearts, arrows
Halftone dot patterns for texture
Diagonal stripe patterns
Illustrated stickers/badges
Cartoon style icons
Geometric patterns (checkerboard, polka dots, zigzags)

Examples of Correct Interactions:
Bad (AI vibes):

Hovering over a game card makes it glow softly with a purple aura and lift slightly

Good (Fun vibes):

Hovering over a game makes the entire section snap to a different bright color instantly, maybe a playful illustration pops in

Bad:

Dark background with semi-transparent cards that have subtle gradients

Good:

Bright yellow section with bold black text, thick black border on the bottom, hand-drawn stars scattered around

Bad:

Button with gradient background that glows on hover

Good:

Button is solid HOT PINK with thick black border. On hover it becomes ELECTRIC YELLOW and the text turns black. Instant change. Maybe it tilts 2 degrees.

The Test:
If someone screenshots this app and it looks like it could be ChatGPT, Claude, Notion AI, or any other AI tool - you failed. It should look like a 90s Nickelodeon show meets a modern indie game meets a colorful museum meets a party flyer.
DO NOT USE:

Dark backgrounds as primary
Subtle grays or muted tones
Glassmorphism or blur
Perfect geometric boxes everywhere
Subtle shadows and glows
Smooth elegant transitions

DO USE:

Bright, saturated solid color backgrounds
Hand-drawn illustration elements
Thick black borders and outlines
Bold drop shadows (not glows)
Organic shapes (blobs, curves, irregular forms)
Patterns and textures (dots, stripes, checkerboard)
Playful doodles and cartoon icons
Color blocking (full sections in solid bright colors)
Typography at extreme scales
Thick borders separating everything

Overall Vibe:
90s Nickelodeon meets modern museum meets indie game meets party flyer. Energetic, colorful, hand-crafted feeling, NOT digital/AI/corporate. Think Jackbox Party Pack, Poolsuite, old Spotify Wrapped pages - bold graphics, playful but sophisticated.
Make it look FUN and BOLD and DIFFERENT. If someone saw this and thought 'that looks fun' instead of 'that looks professional' - you nailed it."
