Key elements from this 'Wallen' design reference:
Color Blocking:

Bold, bright solid color sections (bright yellow, hot pink, electric orange, bright blue)
High saturation colors, nothing muted or pastel
Each section/card gets its own distinct bright color
Color combinations are loud and energetic (yellow + pink, orange + blue)

Typography:

THICK, BOLD, all-caps text with heavy outlines
White text with black outlines/strokes for maximum impact
Text sits directly on bright colored backgrounds
Mix of huge scale and smaller text
Condensed bold fonts for headlines

Layout Style:

Bento box/grid layout with different sized colorful rectangles
Thick black borders separating all sections (3-5px)
Sections are different sizes and proportions (not uniform)
Everything contained in frames/boxes with thick borders
No floating elements or subtle shadows

Sticker/Badge Elements:

Cartoon stickers overlaid on design (smiley faces, flowers, badges)
Thick black outlines on all illustrated elements
Mix of illustrated and photographic elements
Playful icons and graphics scattered throughout
Everything has that bold outlined 'sticker' quality

Y2K/Gen Z energy
